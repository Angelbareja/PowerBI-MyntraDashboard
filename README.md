## 📌 Project Overview

The **Myntra Product & Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The project focuses on analyzing product performance, sales revenue, order distribution, inventory, brand performance, and customer order trends.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand sales performance, identify top-performing products and brands, and evaluate revenue across different city tiers.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue and order performance.
- Understand product and category distribution.
- Evaluate brand-wise revenue performance.
- Analyze size-wise product inventory.
- Identify the top 10 products based on revenue.
- Understand order status distribution.
- Compare revenue across different city tiers.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The project contains two interactive dashboard pages.

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard

This dashboard provides an overview of overall sales performance and order distribution.

**Key Features:**
- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Revenue trends by order date
- Order trends by date
- City-tier-wise order distribution
- Category-wise order analysis
- Brand-wise order performance
- Interactive filters for Brand Name, Category, and City Tier

**Key Business Metrics:**
- Total Revenue: 1.19 Billion
- Total Orders: 180K

### 2. Myntra Product Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on product-level analysis, inventory distribution, and brand performance.

**Key Features:**
- Product distribution by category
- Size-wise inventory analysis
- Brand performance based on revenue
- City-tier-wise revenue analysis
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Order status distribution
- Product quantity and net revenue analysis

**Key Business Metrics:**
- Total Products: 450K
- Total Orders: 180K

## 📈 Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps explore the following business insights:

1. **Revenue Performance:** Evaluate overall revenue and understand sales trends over time.
2. **Brand Analysis:** Compare revenue and order performance across different brands.
3. **Product Performance:** Identify products generating the highest net revenue.
4. **Inventory Analysis:** Understand the distribution of inventory across different product sizes.
5. **Category Analysis:** Identify categories with higher order volumes.
6. **City Tier Analysis:** Compare revenue and order contribution across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities.
7. **Order Status:** Analyze delivered, cancelled, shipped, and returned orders.
